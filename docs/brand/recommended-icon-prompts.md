## road-decision

Use case: logo-brand. Create one outstanding bespoke desktop app icon for AutoKira, a tool that helps Malaysian drivers compare electric and petrol ownership costs and choose confidently. Design direction: road becoming a confident decision. Invent a single coherent sculptural ribbon glyph that reads as a graceful checkmark and a roadway at once: a short lower-left segment turns smoothly upward into a longer upper-right segment, using two broad parallel lanes separated by one narrow continuous negative-space channel. Beautiful custom silhouette, soft aerodynamic ends, expressive but precise proportions, no literal car. The glyph is deep petrol-green satin enamel with a restrained jade edge accent, very shallow layered thickness and subtle soft natural contact shadow. Place it centered on an almost full-canvas gently rounded square cool porcelain-white tile with smooth matte finish, only 4% transparent margin around tile. Pale cool white background inside tile, not beige. A small gentle highlight defines the bevel, no strong specular shine; use sculptural craft, not heavy skeuomorphism. Slight depth, predominantly frontal orthographic app-icon composition, considered whitespace, glyph takes 56-62% width. The icon must be handsome, memorable and immediately legible as a desktop dock icon at 32px; prioritize ownable graphic silhouette over rendering effects. This should look like a professionally commissioned identity for a thoughtful driving decision tool, not generic map, checkbox UI, navigation arrow or racing game. No letters, brand name, digits, tick-circle outline, plus sign, calculator keys, speedometer, dotted paths, endpoint circles, bolts, money bags, photo, logo mockup board, heavy extrusion, grain, glossy plastic, glow, neon or distracting metallic glints. ONE standalone icon. Genuine transparent outside corners.

## cost-motion

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE exceptional bespoke desktop app icon for a driving-cost comparison tool. A distinctive abstract coin-and-road symbol: one bold circular jade disc with a single elegant rising curved roadway cut out through its center, sweeping from lower-left to upper-right; the cut is broad enough to read at 32px and separates the disc into two optically balanced interlocking pieces, subtly expressing comparison of two choices. No literal currency sign, no letter, no split pie chart, no dots or arrows. Make the two pieces slightly different shades of sophisticated jade and deep teal, with a delicate satin ceramic relief and barely visible bevel; a restrained sliver of cool silver is visible only along one inner edge, giving beautiful material depth. Center this compact circular symbol with confident generous whitespace on a near-black charcoal gently rounded-square matte tile, almost full-canvas with 4% transparent outer margin. Calm top-left light, fine clean surfaces WITHOUT grain, short soft contact shadows, subtle optical depth, graphic simplicity, slightly rich luminous jade material but no glow. Modern premium application identity: polished, quiet and ownable. Straight-on frontal composition. Symbol occupies 60% tile width. No text, numeral, speedometer, vehicle silhouette, calculator keys, letters, money bags, bolts, extra decorative symbols, border framing, glossy plastic, chrome, extreme 3D, neon, glitter, illustration scene, mockup or multiple icons. Deliver a single complete app icon with true transparent outside corners.
